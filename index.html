Advocate

Advocate is an Android app that listens to the world around you through your phone or headset microphone, streams what it hears to the AI provider you choose with your own API key, and speaks or shows short, timely advice, information and translation as you go about your day.

Write what you want in plain words, press Start, lock the phone and put it away. Advocate keeps working from a foreground service, speaks into your earpiece and writes to the screen and the lock-screen notification.
